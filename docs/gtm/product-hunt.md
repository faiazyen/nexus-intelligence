## Description

NEXUS monitors 10,000+ daily signals: job posts, SEC filings, leadership
changes, funding rounds, procurement notices. It scores every account on
urgency, fit, and budget probability, then hands you outreach written for the
exact moment the buyer is living through. Enterprise intent intelligence at
$299/month instead of $25K/year.
