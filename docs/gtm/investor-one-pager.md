# NEXUS Intelligence: Investor One-Pager

**Know who's about to buy. Before anyone else does.**

## Problem

By the time a B2B vendor sees an RFP, the deal is already decided. The buying
decision is shaped 4 to 8 months earlier, in meetings the vendor was never in.
70%+ of the B2B buying journey happens in this dark funnel, invisible to
current tools.

The intent-data industry knows this and still fails its customers: 91% of B2B
marketers buy intent data, only 24% see meaningful ROI. The unsolved problem is
the signal-to-action gap. Tools show data and leave the user to figure out what
to do with it.

Meanwhile, the entire product category is priced for enterprises ($25K to
$120K+ per year), leaving consultants, agencies, and B2B service firms, the
people who live and die by being early, with nothing.

## Solution

NEXUS is the first platform that closes the full loop:

1. **Monitor** 10,000+ daily signals: job posts, SEC filings, leadership changes, funding, procurement notices, earnings language, tech-stack changes, news
2. **Reason** about each signal through the lens of the customer's specific business (Claude-powered, grounded in their past wins and ICP)
3. **Score** every account on urgency, fit, and budget probability; only 70+ reaches the queue
4. **Act** with signal-specific outreach in three frames, ready to send in one click

Plus NEXUS BRAIN: a strategic advisor that learns the customer's business and
delivers daily briefings, deal coaching, and proposal drafts.
