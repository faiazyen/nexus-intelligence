# Cold Email Sequence: NEXUS Selling NEXUS

Target: solo consultants and boutique agency owners ($250K to $2M/year in B2B
services). Every send is triggered by a real observable signal about the
recipient, never a blast. Placeholders in {braces}.

Rules applied throughout: subject 7 words max, body 3 sentences max, one small
ask, no product pitch in email 1, no banned phrases, no em dashes.

---

## Email 1: Day 1 (the signal)

**Subject:** The {competitor} RFP you never saw

**Body:**
Saw that {target company} just posted for a {role}, which usually means budget
was approved for a problem nobody has solved yet. Firms like yours typically
hear about these 4 months later, when the RFP is already shaped around someone
else. Worth 15 minutes this week to show you the three buyers in your niche
making that same move right now?

**Why this works:** it demonstrates the product instead of describing it. The
reader experiences being early.

---

## Email 2: Day 4 (the cost frame)

**Subject:** What waiting for referrals actually costs

**Body:**
Quick math on {target company}'s niche: three companies showed buying signals in
the last 30 days, and each engagement in that space runs $40K to $200K. If even
one shortlists a competitor because they called first, that is a six-figure
miss. Can I send you this month's signal list for your exact ICP, no call
needed?

**Why this works:** shifts from curiosity to loss. The ask is smaller than email
1 (a list, not a meeting), which converts fence-sitters.

---

## Email 3: Day 9 (the proof)

**Subject:** How {peer firm} got in pre-RFP

**Body:**
A {niche} consultancy started working leadership-change signals and booked four
first meetings in three weeks, all with companies that had no open RFP. The
window after a new executive lands is about 90 days, and it is the cheapest
meeting you will ever book. Want the two accounts in your market that entered
that window this week?

**Why this works:** social proof plus a concrete, expiring artifact. The offer
is specific to their market, so ignoring it has a visible cost.

---

## Email 4: Day 16 (the breakup)

**Subject:** Closing your file

**Body:**
I will stop here since timing might be wrong, and that is fair. One parting
data point: the two accounts I flagged for your niche are still inside their
buying window for roughly 40 more days. If you want the list before I close it
out, reply with one word and it is yours.

**Why this works:** breakup emails routinely pull the highest reply rate in the
sequence, and the expiring window gives a real reason to answer today.

---

## Sequence notes

- Send Tuesday to Thursday, 8:40 to 10:20am recipient time.
- Any reply kills the sequence and routes to a human.
- Every claim about signals must be true and generated from NEXUS itself. The
  sequence is the demo.
